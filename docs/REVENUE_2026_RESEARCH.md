# AUREUS-2026 Revenue Research Baseline

## Terminology

`Web4` is not treated as a standardized protocol. In AUREUS it is only an internal label for the convergence of autonomous agents, machine-readable services, identity/authorization, programmable payments and evidence-backed digital commerce.

## 2026 signals incorporated

- Agentic commerce is becoming a real payment category: Visa and Mastercard publicly describe agent-authorized purchasing/payment flows with identity, limits and accountability.
- MCP is expanding beyond tool access into commercial publishing/marketplace workflows; Microsoft exposes a Marketplace Ingestion MCP server for creating, validating and publishing commercial offers.
- Machine-to-machine services and API micropayments are emerging around protocols such as x402. AUREUS treats these as experimental seller-side channels until legal, settlement, custody and authorization controls are verified.
- AI-enabled professional services remain a high-priority path to first cash because they require less capital than compute/mining and can be delivered through authorized marketplace channels.

## Revenue channels

1. AI integration and automation services.
2. AI video/creator production services.
3. Agent/API microservices sold per request, subscription or outcome.
4. MCP-enabled commercial marketplace offers.
5. Data and knowledge products with verified rights/provenance.
6. Open-source sponsorships and support contracts.
7. GPU/compute sell-side revenue.
8. Authorized bug-bounty work inside explicit scope.
9. Agentic machine-to-machine services, seller-side first.

## Compounding rule

AUREUS may compound only `VERIFIED_NET_CASH_SETTLED`. Platform balances, invoices, pending payouts, rewards, credits, token emissions or simulated P&L are not reinvestable profit.

Default portfolio policy:

- >=50% reserve floor.
- <=25% reinvestment.
- <=5% experimental allocation.
- no borrowing or leverage.
- any new capital commitment requires human approval.

## Production evidence

A channel cannot become production-authorized merely because an adapter or model exists. Each channel must progress through:

`IMPLEMENTED -> CI_VERIFIED -> RUNTIME_VERIFIED -> ECONOMICALLY_VERIFIED`

Final `PRODUCTION_GO` additionally requires no unauthorized financial actions/provider writes, >=95% required telemetry coverage, complete attributable costs, and at least three independent positive settlement windows under the current internal policy.
